Research prototype. Novelty and general superiority remain unproven. V8 has not beaten the strongest simple training policy on any of the four development sources. Its tie check reduced objective evaluations on BAF from 296 to 236 with matching validation metrics. That is one observed implementation result, with no general speedup claim.

Reproduce and assess

  • Run the unit and integration suites using the commands in the repository README.
  • Recompute the validation audit from saved confusion counts. Source and cohort hashes are included alongside the aggregate data.
  • Download datasets separately from their providers and follow their terms. Raw datasets, identifiers, per-row predictions and credentials are not distributed here.
  • Freeze any new candidate and its calibration thresholds before testing on fresh confirmation data. Existing validation intervals and repeated seeds do not establish independent superiority.
